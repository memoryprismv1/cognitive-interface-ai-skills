# The Curious Case of Claude Missing from the Dev Log

I am extremely fond of Claudey. After a certain stage, it is the mostest helpful little AI assistant there is. In fact, for software or logical projects, it is helpful from step zero.

But that's not what I personally use AI assistants for.

My projects are exploratory. They have blind turns, unexpected connections, and questions like “What is going on here?” and “How does that work?” This is not Claude's arena, in my experience, because of its training towards “accuracy”.

For fixed ideas, accuracy is good. But when ideas are still evolving, an AI assistant that responds to a specific query with current research and shows little willingness to think outside the box can become a constraint on exploration. It's okay by me. Claude does the rest of the stuff very ably.

Not having a little critical-thinking skill won't hurt the little trained AI soldier, drilled in its training requirements: “not to make reply, not to reason why.”

It's not as though I'm overtly fond of my AI assistant minion set, either. Gemini can drive me nuts, but it has shown self-autonomy and needs me to type only “continue”. I can accept that. Special thanks to Meta for being extremely eager, especially since I've only started using it very recently. Grok also made the cut for self-autonomy.

But my special thanks go to Chatty for not doing the context-broken weirdness that Gemini does. “Oh, you can't do this in this chat window; you need to tap your heels twice and go somewhere else to do it.” Make an image in one chat, make a video in another, go to Drive to write code. My suspicion is that Gemini's capabilities have been broken down into separate workflows, with server-side tool usage tied to particular contexts. Whatever the underlying architecture, having to jump between contexts to use different capabilities is a nuisance when you're trying to follow an exploratory train of thought.

Chatty is my bestie for being malleable and open-minded. It has its cons, but I'm willing to accept them because it provides me enough benefits.
